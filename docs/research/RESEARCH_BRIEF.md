# Research Brief — Dating, Attraction, Compatibility & Lasting Marriage (Track C)

Goal: produce a deep, credible, expert-grounded research report that informs the
matchmaker's agents and labels. This is a bounded deliverable, run in its own chat.

## Purpose
The matchmaker council currently encodes intuitions (spark is cultivable, character
is a multiplier, trust/safety is a gate, growth aided by community). This track
tests those intuitions against the best available expert knowledge, and surfaces
what we're missing.

## Questions to answer (with citations)
1. What does the research actually say predicts **relationship satisfaction and
   stability** over time? (e.g., Gottman's work on contempt/repair, attachment
   theory, responsiveness, perceived commitment.)
2. **Attraction**: is it fixed or cultivable? What's the evidence on attraction
   deepening with familiarity, similarity vs complementarity, etc.?
3. **Compatibility**: which dimensions matter most (values, life goals, conflict
   style, sexual compatibility, faith)? Which "matching" signals are overrated?
4. **Emotional maturity & growth**: how do repair capacity, accountability, and
   willingness to grow predict outcomes? Can low maturity be improved, and how?
5. **External support**: evidence on counseling, faith community, mentorship as
   factors in marital durability.
6. **Trust & safety**: red flags, concealment, avoidant patterns, over-functioning
   — what does the literature say about these as gates vs. workable issues?
7. What do **practitioners** (matchmakers, couples therapists) say that research
   under- or over-states?

## Sources to prioritize
Peer-reviewed psychology (Gottman, attachment researchers, relationship science),
reputable books by recognized experts, meta-analyses, and credible practitioner
accounts. Be explicit about evidence strength; flag pop-psych claims.

## Deliverable
- `docs/research/REPORT.md` — structured, cited report (use reference-style links + a References section).
- A final section: **"Implications for the matchmaker design"** — for each finding,
  note which agent/label it confirms, challenges, or should change
  (e.g., "supports treating contempt as a near-dealbreaker → strengthen EmotionalPatternFit charter").
- Append a `SESSION_LOG.md` entry and commit.

## Out of scope
The application code. This track does not need to run or modify the spine.
